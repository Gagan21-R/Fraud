          FraudShield AI

          An AI-powered fraud detection system that analyses every card transaction against the customer's own behaviour, scores its risk in milliseconds, and explains why it looks suspicious — so banks can stop fraud without blocking genuine customers.

          The problem

          Digital payments in India cross billions of transactions every month, and card / UPI fraud grows with them. Fixed rules like "block anything over ₹50,000" miss clever fraud (many small "test" charges) and annoy honest customers (a big but genuine purchase). Fraud is also rare — often under 2% of transactions — so a naive model that says "never fraud" is 98% accurate and completely useless. FraudShield AI learns what normal looks like for each customer and flags what doesn't fit.

          How the AI works — pipeline

          1. 1Transaction dataAmount, merchant, location, time, device, channel
          2. 2Feature engineering11 behavioural signals: spend ratio, velocity, distance, new device…
          3. 3Supervised AILogistic Regression learns from labelled fraud cases
          4. 4Unsupervised AIIsolation Forest spots never-seen-before anomalies
          5. 5Hybrid risk score80% LR + 20% IF → score from 0 to 100
          6. 6Explain & decideApprove / Review / Block with human-readable reasons

          AI techniques used

          Logistic Regression (supervised ML)
          Trained from scratch with the Adam optimiser, L2 regularisation and class weighting to handle the rare-fraud imbalance. Its weights tell us which behaviours matter most.
          Isolation Forest (unsupervised ML)
          An ensemble of random trees that isolates unusual points. Fraud is "few and different", so it gets isolated in fewer splits. It needs no labels, so it can catch brand-new attack patterns.
          Ensemble learning
          The two models fail differently: Logistic Regression is precise on fraud patterns it has seen, while Isolation Forest flags anything unusual, even attacks it was never shown. Blending them gives one balanced, robust score. The AI Model Lab compares all three on the same unseen test data.
          Explainable AI (XAI)
          Each feature's contribution to the log-odds is shown as a bar chart and turned into plain-English reason codes.
          Threshold optimisation
          The blocking threshold is tuned automatically to maximise the recall-weighted F2 score (a missed fraud costs more than a false alarm), and analysts can adjust it live.

          Features of the system

          • Dashboard — KPIs, daily trends, category and hourly risk charts, auto-generated insights
          • Live Monitor — real-time stream with instant approve / review / block and attack simulation
          • Analyzer — score any what-if transaction, with a risk gauge and reason codes
          • Transactions — search, filter, sort and review every scored payment, confirm or clear cases
          • Batch Scan — upload a CSV and screen thousands of rows at once, export results
          • AI Model Lab — accuracy, precision, recall, F1, ROC-AUC, confusion matrix, feature importance, retraining
          • Neumorphic UI — soft 3-D interface with light and dark themes, fully responsive

          Benefits

          • Stops losses early — fraud is blocked before money leaves the account
          • Fewer false alarms — judges each customer against their own habits, not one fixed rule
          • Catches new attacks — the anomaly detector needs no prior examples
          • Transparent — every decision comes with reasons, which supports RBI-style audit and customer disputes
          • Fast & scalable — scoring takes under a millisecond per transaction
          • Tunable — the bank chooses its own balance of security and convenience

          Fraud patterns simulated

          🌍 Account takeover

          A criminal logs in from a new device, often abroad and at night, and makes several high-value purchases at risky merchants.

          💳 Card testing

          A quick burst of tiny online charges to check if stolen card details work, followed by one large cash-out.

          🧾 Stolen card

          A physical card is used in shops for expensive electronics or jewellery — the hardest case, often in the victim's own city.
